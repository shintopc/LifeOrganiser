Life Organiser - Your Ultimate Personal Productivity & Management Companion
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. APP DETAILS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Life Organiser is a robust, secure, and all-in-one productivity application 
designed to help you manage every aspect of your life efficiently. From tracking 
finances to securing your passwords and organizing daily tasks, Life Organiser 
provides a unified platform with a focus on privacy and user experience.

Key Features:
- Biometric & PIN Security for app-wide protection.
- Privacy-first design (screenshots and recent apps preview disabled).
- Flexible Theme Support (Light, Dark, and System).
- Modular design allowing you to manage and toggle features as needed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. USER GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Getting Started:
- Secure Your Data: Upon first launch, set up your PIN or Biometric lock in the 
  Security settings to ensure your sensitive data remains private.
- Personalize: Visit the Profile section to add your details and customize the 
  app's appearance in Settings.
- Manage Modules: Use the "Manage Modules" screen to enable or disable specific 
  features based on your needs.

Navigation:
- Dashboard (Home): Get a bird's-eye view of your day, including upcoming tasks 
  and quick shortcuts.
- Hub: Access all your modules from a central location.
- Quick Actions: Use the floating action buttons (where available) to quickly 
  add tasks, notes, or records.

Best Practices:
- Regularly back up your important documents in the Documents module.
- Use the Finance module daily to maintain an accurate budget.
- Set high-priority tasks in the Tasks module to stay focused on what matters.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. MODULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Life Organiser is divided into several specialized modules:

- [Tasks]: Create, organize, and track your daily to-do lists. Includes a 
  calendar view for long-term planning and priority management.
- [Reminders]: Never miss an important event. Set time-sensitive reminders 
  with notification alerts.
- [Finance]: A comprehensive financial tracker. Manage loans, track expenses, 
  monitor budgets, and visualize your spending habits.
- [Notes]: A digital notebook for your thoughts, ideas, and quick checklists.
- [Passwords]: A secure vault for your login credentials. Data is encrypted 
  and protected by your app lock.
- [Documents]: Digitalize and organize your physical documents. Store and 
  view files directly within the app.
- [Emergency]: Keep critical information at your fingertips. Store emergency 
  contacts and basic medical details for quick access.
- [Security]: Manage your app's protection settings, including PIN changes, 
  biometric toggles, and auto-lock behavior.
- [Profile]: View and edit your personal information and account details.
- [Settings]: Global app configurations, including theme selection, 
  notification preferences, and module management.
